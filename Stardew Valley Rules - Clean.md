# Stardew Valley: The Board Game — Clean Rules Reference

This is a cleaned and reorganized English reference based on the rulebook in `stardew rules.pdf`. It preserves the rules in a format that is easier to search and read, but the original PDF remains the authority for card art, symbols, diagrams, and edge cases tied to specific components.

## 1. Objective

*Stardew Valley: The Board Game* is a cooperative game. The players win immediately when they complete the objectives required by their chosen difficulty.

### Standard game: Honest Farmer

Before the Season Deck runs out, the group must:

1. Complete all 4 revealed Grandpa's Goals.
2. Restore all 6 Community Center Rooms by completing 1 Bundle for each room.

If the final Season Card is drawn before the objectives are complete, Joja wins.

### Difficulty modes

- **Seedling:** Complete the 4 Grandpa's Goals only.
- **Honest Farmer:** Complete 4 Grandpa's Goals and all 6 Community Center Rooms.
- **Artisan:** Complete the Honest Farmer objectives and remove every Joja Tile from the board.

## 2. Setup

### 2.1 Basic components

Place the board in the center of the table. Put the following nearby:

- 3 Stardew Dice
- 3 Animal Dice
- Spouse Pawn
- Pet/Starting Player Token
- Gold and Heart Tokens

Shuffle these decks and place them facedown:

- Villagers
- Items
- Epic Items
- Events
- Mine Events
- Joja Tiles

### 2.2 Tile tray and bags

Place the Tile Tray near the board. It contains crops, animal products, materials, and other common resources.

- Put all Artifact and Mineral Tiles in the gray Artifact/Mineral Bag.
- Put all fishing-related tiles in the blue Fishing Bag.

### 2.3 Starting crop

Place 1 Parsnip in the second field slot, normal side up.

Crops are double-sided:

- Front: normal
- Back: Quality

### 2.4 Forageables and trees

- Mix the 11 Spring Forageable Tiles.
- Put 1 facedown Forageable on each Foraging Spot.
- Put the 4 Spring Tree Tiles on the Tree Spots, leaf side up.
- Keep the remaining seasonal Forageables and Trees nearby.

### 2.5 Fish Track

Draw 5 random tiles from the Fishing Bag and place them on the Fish Track. When filling empty spaces, always begin with the rightmost empty slot.

### 2.6 Mine

- Sort Mine Level Cards from Level 1 on top to Level 12 on the bottom.
- Place the stack faceup on the Mine Level space.
- Shuffle the Map Cards.
- Reveal 1 Map Card and place it beside the Mine Levels.

### 2.7 Season Deck

For a first game, use only the 16 Standard Season Cards.

1. Separate them into Spring, Summer, Fall, and Winter.
2. Shuffle the 4 cards within each season.
3. Put each season's 4 cards on top of its matching Season End Card.
4. Stack the season piles in calendar order: Spring, Summer, Fall, Winter.

The completed deck contains 20 cards.

### 2.8 Community Center

For each of the 6 Community Center Rooms:

1. Randomly choose 1 Bundle Card matching that room.
2. Place it facedown on the room.
3. Do not look at it.

Set the unused Bundles aside. They may be needed if a Bundle is replaced.

### 2.9 Grandpa's Goals

Shuffle the Goal Deck, reveal 4 Goals, and place them on Grandpa's Letter.

### 2.10 Animals and buildings

- Separate Coop Animals and Barn Animals into 2 stacks.
- Put the Coop Building on the Coop Animal stack.
- Put the Barn Building on the Barn Animal stack.
- Randomly reveal 2 additional Building Tiles.
- Return the other Buildings to the box.

### 2.11 Player setup

Each player:

1. Chooses a Player Mat and its Profession.
2. Takes the matching pawn.
3. Chooses 1 Starting Tool Deck:
   - Watering Can
   - Hoe
   - Fishing Rod
   - Pickaxe
4. Places that tool's cards on the Player Mat with Level 0 faceup on top.

### 2.12 Starting player and shared resources

- The person who suggested playing chooses the Starting Player.
- Give that player the Pet Token.
- The group chooses the cat or dog side.
- The team starts with **3 shared Gold total**, regardless of player count.

## 3. Goals and Bundles

## 3.1 Grandpa's Goals

Four Goals are revealed at setup. All revealed Goals must be completed unless using a shortened variant.

Possible Goals include:

- Have 10 Gold per player.
- Reach Mine Level 12.
- Donate 2 Museum items per player.
- Have 2 animals per player.
- Have 3 friends per player.
- Catch 1 Legendary Fish per player.
- Build 1 Building per player.
- Upgrade Starting Tools 2 times per player.

Requirements marked with the player-count symbol are multiplied by the number of players.

The group may satisfy scaled Goals collectively. For example, the friendship Goal does not require every individual player to own the same number of friends.

## 3.2 Community Center Bundles

There are 6 rooms. To restore the Community Center, complete 1 Bundle belonging to each room.

A Bundle normally requires a number of donations equal to the number of players.

### Room themes

- **Crafts Room:** Seasonal Forageables or combinations of materials.
- **Pantry:** Crops, animal products, and Quality farm goods.
- **Fish Tank:** Fish from specified locations or of specified types.
- **Bulletin Board:** Resources plus Heart Tokens.
- **Vault:** Gold donations.
- **Boiler Room:** Mine resources such as Ore, Minerals, and Bug Meat.

### Important Bundle rules

- Legendary Fish may be donated to any fishing Bundle.
- Bulletin Board progress requires both the listed resource and 1 Heart Token per player.
- Vault amounts are multiplied by the number of players.
- Donations may be made over multiple actions; leave donated resources on the Bundle to track progress.

## 4. Round Structure

Every round has 3 phases:

1. **Season Phase**
2. **Planning Phase**
3. **Action Phase**

After every player has completed a turn, begin a new round.

## 4.1 Season Phase

The Starting Player draws the top Season Card and resolves it from top to bottom.

### Common Season Card effects

- **Quality Crop:** Flip any planted crop to its Quality side.
- **Rain:** Move every planted crop 1 field space to the right. Harvest crops that leave the track.
- **Fish Move:** Remove the 2 rightmost Fish Tiles, shift the others right, and refill empty slots. Legendary Fish removed this way return to the Fishing Bag.
- **Gift:** Every player may trigger the Gift Ability of 1 befriended Villager. Also trigger Gift Abilities on applicable non-Villager cards.
- **Pet Wanders:** Pass the Pet Token to the player on the left.
- **Green Crow:** Discard 1 crop planted in a green field.
- **Red Crow:** Discard 1 crop planted in a red field.
- **Event:** Draw and resolve an Event Card.
- **Joja:** Draw a Joja Tile and place it on the corresponding board action.
- **Shipping Bin:** Players may trade and sell resources.

Field 3 counts as both red and green for Crow effects.

Festival Cards have their own written effect. Treat Festivals like Regular Season Cards when constructing a randomized Season Deck.

## 4.2 Planning Phase

Players may:

- Discuss their plans.
- Trade Item Cards and resource tiles freely.
- Place each Player Pawn at any location.

Initial placement during Planning is not movement and therefore does not allow foraging.

After the Action Phase begins, players may change their intended action but cannot freely change their starting location.

## 4.3 Action Phase

The Starting Player takes a full turn, followed clockwise by the other players.

On a turn, choose one structure:

- **Action + Action** at the current location, with no normal movement.
- **Action + Move + Action**, moving exactly 1 path between the actions.

A player is not required to use every action.

After completing the turn:

1. Return the pawn to the Farmhouse.
2. Choose up to 1 type of End of Turn Effect.
3. Repeat that selected effect as many times as the player can pay for it.

The Spouse Pawn follows separate rules and does not trigger End of Turn Effects.

---

## 5. Movement and Foraging

Whenever a player moves along a path, they may take either:

- 1 facedown Forageable Tile adjacent to that path, or
- 1 Tree Tile adjacent to that path.

This is automatic, optional, and does not use an action.

### Forageables

Reveal the chosen tile and place it in inventory.

If it instructs the player to draw an Artifact or Mineral:

1. Check the icon on the tile.
2. Discard the Forageable Tile.
3. Draw the specified resource type from the Artifact/Mineral Bag.

### Trees

Claimed Tree Tiles become Wood. Trees do not count as Forageables.

## 6. Locations and Actions

### Farm

- Water Crops
- Collect from Animals

### Ranch

### Town

- Buy and Plant Seeds
- Make a Friend
- Reveal and Donate to Bundles

### Mountain

- Explore the Mine
- Buy Buildings

### Forge

- Open Geodes
- Donate to the Museum

### Forest

- River Fishing

### Beach

- Ocean Fishing

## 7. Farm Actions

## 7.1 Water Crops

Spend 1 action to move every planted crop 1 field space to the right.

- A crop that moves off the track is harvested and added to inventory.
- If inventory is full, discard another resource first or discard the harvested crop.
- Crops do not wither when seasons change.
- Crops can be destroyed by Crow effects.

## 7.2 Collect from Animals

Spend 1 action and roll all 3 Animal Dice.

Every matching die activates every owned animal with that symbol once. Each activation generates the resource shown on that Animal Tile.

Example: If the group owns 2 cows and rolls 2 Cow symbols, each cow activates twice, producing 4 Milk total.

### Happy animals

Animals on their Happy side generate Quality animal products. Use the Pet Animals End of Turn Effect to make an animal Happy.

---

## 8. Ranch Action: Buy Animals

Spend 1 action to buy any number of affordable animals.

- The group must own the animal's required Building, normally a Coop or Barn.
- Pay the Gold cost shown on each Animal Tile.
- Animals and Buildings are shared by the whole group.

---

## 9. Town Actions

## 9.1 Buy and Plant Seeds

Spend 1 action at Pierre's General Store.

- Each Crop Tile costs 1 Gold.
- Only crops from the current season may be bought.
- Purchased crops are planted immediately.
- Place each crop in an empty field matching the watering number printed on the crop.
- Normally, only 1 crop may occupy each field slot.

During this same action, players may sell resources for the Gold value printed on them. Buying, planting, and selling may be done in any order.

## 9.2 Make a Friend

Spend 1 action and reveal the top Villager Card.

- If the player gives an acceptable gift, keep the Villager as a friend and gain Hearts.
- If the player cannot or does not want to gift the Villager, discard the card.

### Gift results

- **Loved gift:** Gain 2 Hearts.
- **Liked gift:** Gain 1 Heart.
- **Hated gift:** Cannot be given.
- If the current season is the Villager's birthday season, gain 1 additional Heart.

Any giftable resource not specifically shown as Loved or Hated counts as Liked. Resources marked with the No Gift symbol cannot be gifted.

When a Gift symbol appears on a Season Card, each player may activate the Gift Ability of 1 of their Villager friends.

## 9.3 Reveal and Donate to Bundles

Spend 1 action at the Community Center. During that action, reveal, replace, and donate as much as the group can afford.

### Revealing or replacing a Bundle

Pay Hearts equal to the number of players, then choose one:

- Reveal the leftmost facedown Bundle, or
- Replace a revealed Bundle with another Bundle matching the same room.

If a Bundle with donated resources is replaced, discard all progress on it.

### Completing a Bundle

- Donate the required resources.
- Unless specified otherwise, the number of donations equals the number of players.
- Partial donations remain on the card.
- When complete, discard or return the donated resources and remove the Bundle.
- The room is restored.

### Bundle rewards

- Each of the first 5 completed Bundles grants 1 regular Item.
- The final completed Bundle grants 1 Epic Item.
- Give the reward to any player.

---

## 10. Forge Actions

## 10.1 Open Geodes

Spend 1 action to open any number of Geodes.

For each Geode:

1. Roll 1 Stardew Die.
2. Consult the Geode chart on the board.
3. Gain the indicated result, such as Stone, Ore, a Mineral, an Artifact, or an Item.
4. Discard the Geode. If it becomes Ore, it may simply be flipped to its Ore side.

## 10.2 Donate to the Museum

Spend 1 action to donate any number of Artifacts or Minerals.

- Place each tile in an empty Museum slot matching its letter, A–H.
- A tile marked `?` may occupy any empty letter slot.
- Gain 1 Heart for every tile donated.
- Filling each Museum column grants 1 Epic Item, for a maximum of 2 Epic Items.

---

## 11. Mountain Actions

## 11.1 Buy Buildings

Spend 1 action at Robin's shop.

- Pay the Gold and materials printed on the Building Tile.
- Multiple Buildings may be bought with one action if all costs can be paid.
- Buildings are shared by all players.

## 11.2 Explore the Mine

Spend 1 action and roll 2 Stardew Dice.

Use one die as the Map Card's row and the other as its column. The player chooses which die controls which axis. Resolve the matching grid space.

Possible results include:

- Gain Stone.
- Gain Bug Meat.
- Gain available Ore.
- Gain an available Geode.
- Descend 1 Mine Level.
- Gain an Item.
- Trigger the current Monster Ability.
- Draw and resolve a Mine Event.

The current Mine Level Card determines:

- Available Ore and Geode types.
- The current Monster Ability.

Whenever the group descends:

1. Reveal the next Mine Level Card.
2. Replace the Map Card with a new one.

If already on Mine Level 12, descending only replaces the Map Card.

---

## 12. Fishing

Fishing is available at:

- Forest: River
- Beach: Ocean
- Mountain: Lake

Spend 1 action at the appropriate location.

## 12.1 Fish Track

A fish can only be caught from a location matching its color and location icon.

After every Fishing Action:

1. Slide remaining tiles right to fill empty spaces.
2. Draw from the Fishing Bag to refill the Track, beginning with the rightmost empty slot.

## 12.2 Catching normal fish

1. Roll all 3 Stardew Dice.
2. Spend rolled Heart, Junimo, and Stardrop symbols to meet catch requirements.
3. Each die can contribute to only 1 fish.
4. Multiple fish may be caught if the dice can satisfy each fish separately.

## 12.3 Legendary Fish

- Caught in the same way as normal fish.
- They are not discarded by Fish Track movement.
- If movement would push one off the Track, return it to the Fishing Bag.
- If sold, gifted, or donated, discard it normally.
- A Legendary Fish may satisfy any fishing Bundle.
- A Legendary Fish Goal only requires the fish to have been caught; it does not need to be kept.

## 12.4 Crab Pot Fish

During a Fishing Action, discard 1 Bug Meat for each Crab Pot Fish taken from the current location.

This is in addition to the normal dice roll, so a player may catch Crab Pot Fish and regular fish in the same action.

## 12.5 Trash

If no fish of any kind is caught during the action, the player may take 1 Trash Tile from the Track.

## 12.6 Treasure Chests

If a player catches the fish immediately to the left of a Treasure Chest Tile:

- Discard the Treasure Chest.
- Gain 1 Item.

## 12.7 Fishing Bag rule

Fishing tiles are normally never returned to the Fishing Bag. The exception is a Legendary Fish removed by Fish Track movement.

---

## 13. End of Turn Effects

After returning to the Farmhouse, choose only 1 effect type. Perform that chosen effect as many times as costs can be paid.

## 13.1 Build Staircase

Discard Stone equal to the number of players to descend 1 Mine Level.

## 13.2 Pet Animals

Pay the number of Hearts printed on an owned Animal Tile to flip it to its Happy side.

## 13.3 Remove Joja

Remove 1 Joja Tile by paying either:

- 1 Heart, or
- 5 Gold.

Do not pay both.

## 13.4 Upgrade Starting Tool

Discard the resource listed on the current Starting Tool Card and advance that tool by 1 level.

---

## 14. Starting Tools, Inventory, and Cards

## 14.1 Starting Tools

Each player's chosen Starting Tool begins at Level 0. Keep its cards stacked faceup in descending level order, with the current level on top.

## 14.2 Normal limits

Each player may normally hold:

- 6 resource tiles
- 2 regular Items
- 2 Profession Upgrades

There is no limit to:

- Epic Items
- Villager friends
- Retained Event Cards

If a player gains a resource with no inventory space, they must discard a resource or discard the newly gained tile.

## 14.3 Trading

Resources and Items may be traded during:

- Planning Phase
- Shipping Bin effects

They cannot normally be traded during the Action Phase.

Profession Upgrades cannot be traded. Epic Items may be traded unless the card says otherwise.

## 14.4 Resource types

The 8 basic resource types are:

- Crops
- Forageables
- Fish
- Animal Products
- Ore
- Geodes
- Artifacts
- Minerals

Other inventory tiles include Trash, Monster Loot, and materials. These generally cannot be gifted to Villagers.

---

## 15. Season End Cards

There is 1 Season End Card for each season.

When drawn:

1. Remove all current Forageables and Trees.
2. Place the next season's Trees and Forageables on the board.
3. Every player draws 2 Profession Upgrades matching their Player Mat.
4. Each player may keep 1 of the 2 cards.
5. A player who already has 2 Profession Upgrades may discard one to make space.
6. Draw the next Season Card immediately.
7. Resolve that new Regular or Festival Card.
8. Continue to Planning and Action Phases as normal.

If a Profession Upgrade is double-sided, choose which side is active when keeping it.

Crops already planted do not wither when the season changes.

---

## 16. Joja Tiles

When a Season Card shows a Joja symbol:

1. Draw a Joja Tile.
2. Reveal its location/action icon.
3. Place it by the corresponding board action.

The tile changes or blocks that action until removed.

Remove a Joja Tile with the Remove Joja End of Turn Effect by paying 1 Heart or 5 Gold.

Gold, Hearts, and Stone are not limited by the physical components supplied in the game.

---

## 17. Marriage

To marry:

1. Gain the Mermaid's Pendant Epic Item.
2. Attach it to a Villager friend displaying the Mermaid's Pendant icon.
3. Take the Spouse Pawn.

During each Planning Phase, place the Spouse Pawn on any location.

During the owning player's turn, the Spouse may take 1 action at its location, either before or after all of the player's own actions.

The Spouse:

- May use the player's inventory, cards, and abilities.
- Does not receive a separate inventory.
- Cannot trigger an End of Turn Effect.

---

## 18. Optional Variants

## 18.1 Randomized Season Deck

For a less predictable game, construct each season by randomly selecting 4 cards from all 20 available cards for that season, then place them above the matching Season End Card.

## 18.2 Easier game

Begin with one or more Community Center Bundles already revealed.

## 18.3 Artisan scoring challenge

- Play on Artisan difficulty.
- Include more Season Cards with Joja symbols.
- If the group wins, its score equals the number of Joja Tiles removed.

## 18.4 Short game

For a shorter game:

- Use 2 Season Cards per season instead of 4, plus each Season End Card.
- Reveal and complete only 2 Grandpa's Goals.
- Randomly choose 3 Community Center Rooms and use 1 facedown Bundle for each.
- The Community Center is restored when those 3 Bundles are complete.

---

## 19. Solo Rules

Solo play follows the normal rules with one Profession change:

At each Season End, instead of drawing only from the Player Mat's Profession, draw 3 Profession Upgrades from any combination of the 4 Profession decks. Keep 1 and discard the others.

---

## 20. Common Rules Clarifications

- Crops in fields do not wither when the season changes.
- Items and resources cannot normally be traded during the Action Phase.
- Trash and Fiber may be discarded at any time.
- The Pet Token moves only when a Season Card instructs it to move.
- The Legendary Fish Goal requires catching the fish, not keeping it.
- Friendship Goal progress may be distributed among players.
- Gold and Heart Tokens are shared by the group.
- Stone, Gold, and Heart Tokens are not limited by component quantity.
- At Mine Level 12, further descents replace only the Map Card.
- Festivals count as Regular Season Cards when building a Season Deck.
- Whenever a tile is gained, it goes into inventory unless immediately discarded.

---

## 21. Quick Turn Reference

### Start of round

1. Draw and resolve a Season Card.
2. Discuss plans, trade, and place pawns.
3. Starting Player takes a turn, followed clockwise.

### Player turn

Choose one:

- Action + Action
- Action + Move + Action

If moving, optionally forage from an adjacent area.

### End of player turn

1. Return the pawn to the Farmhouse.
2. Choose 1 End of Turn Effect type.
3. Repeat it as many times as affordable.

### End of game

- Win immediately when all objectives for the selected mode are complete.
- Lose if the final Season Card is drawn before completing them.
