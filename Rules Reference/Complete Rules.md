# Stardew Valley: The Board Game — Complete Rules

This searchable reference reorganizes the English rulebook. For component illustrations, exact symbols, and card text, consult `../stardew rules.pdf`. Confirmed edge cases are kept separately in `Clarifications and Edge Cases.md`.

## 1. Objective and game end

The game is cooperative. In the standard **Honest Farmer** mode, win immediately by completing both of these before the Season Deck runs out:

1. All 4 revealed Grandpa's Goals.
2. All 6 Community Center Rooms by completing 1 Bundle for each room.

If the final Season Card is drawn before the required objectives are complete, Joja wins.

### Difficulty modes

- **Seedling:** complete the 4 Grandpa's Goals only.
- **Honest Farmer:** complete 4 Goals and all 6 Community Center Rooms.
- **Artisan:** complete the Honest Farmer objectives and leave no Joja Tiles on the board.

## 2. Setup

1. **Board and decks.** Put the board in the center. Place the Stardew Dice, Animal Dice, Spouse Pawn, Gold, and Hearts nearby. Shuffle Villagers, Items, Epic Items, Events, Mine Events, and Joja Tiles into separate facedown decks/stacks.
2. **Supply and bags.** Put the Tile Tray near the board. Put all Artifacts and Minerals in the gray bag and all fishing tiles in the blue Fishing Bag.
3. **Starting crop.** Put 1 normal-side Parsnip in field slot 2. Crop backs are their Quality sides.
4. **Forageables and Trees.** Mix the 11 Spring Forageables and place 1 facedown on every Foraging Spot. Put the 4 Spring Trees on the Tree Spots, leaf side up. Keep later-season tiles nearby.
5. **Fish Track.** Draw 5 random tiles from the Fishing Bag. Fill slots from the rightmost empty slot toward the left.
6. **Mine.** Stack Mine Levels faceup in order, Level 1 on top through Level 12 on the bottom. Shuffle Map Cards and reveal 1 next to the Mine Levels.
7. **Season Deck.** For a first game, separate the 16 Standard Season Cards into 4 seasons. Shuffle each set of 4, put it on top of that season's Season End Card, then stack Spring, Summer, Fall, and Winter in order. This makes a 20-card deck.
8. **Community Center.** For each of the 6 rooms, randomly choose 1 matching Bundle and put it facedown on that room without looking. Set unused Bundles aside.
9. **Grandpa's Goals.** Shuffle and reveal 4 Goals on Grandpa's Letter.
10. **Animals and Buildings.** Make separate Coop Animal and Barn Animal stacks. Put the Coop Building on the Coop stack and the Barn Building on the Barn stack. Randomly reveal 2 other Buildings; return the rest to the box.
11. **Players.** Each player chooses a Player Mat/Profession, matching pawn, and one Starting Tool deck: Watering Can, Hoe, Fishing Rod, or Pickaxe. Put Level 0 faceup on the mat with higher levels beneath it.
12. **Starting Player.** The person who proposed the game chooses the Starting Player and gives them the Pet Token. Choose its cat or dog side. The whole team starts with **3 shared Gold total**.

## 3. Goals and Bundles

### Grandpa's Goals

Possible Goals are:

- Have 10 Gold per player.
- Reach Mine Level 12.
- Donate 2 Artifacts or Minerals to the Museum per player.
- Own 2 animals per player.
- Have 3 Villager friends per player.
- Catch 1 Legendary Fish per player.
- Build 1 Building per player.
- Upgrade Starting Tools 2 times per player.

The player-count symbol means multiply the requirement by the number of players. Requirements are collective totals unless a card explicitly says otherwise.

### Community Center

Each room needs exactly 1 completed Bundle:

- **Crafts Room:** Forageables and materials.
- **Pantry:** Crops, Animal Products, and Quality farm goods.
- **Fish Tank:** Fish from specified locations/types.
- **Bulletin Board:** Listed goods together with Hearts.
- **Vault:** Gold.
- **Boiler Room:** Mine goods such as Ore, Minerals, and Bug Meat.

Unless the card says otherwise, a Bundle needs donations equal to the number of players. Vault amounts are multiplied by player count. Bulletin Board progress requires 1 listed resource plus 1 Heart per player. A Legendary Fish can satisfy any fishing Bundle.

## 4. Round structure

Each round has 3 phases:

1. Season Phase
2. Planning Phase
3. Action Phase

After all players finish their turns, begin the next round.

### 4.1 Season Phase

The Starting Player draws a Season Card and resolves its icons from top to bottom, in order.

- **Quality Crop:** choose any planted crop and flip it to Quality.
- **Rain:** move every planted crop 1 field space to the right. Harvest anything that leaves the track.
- **Fish Move:** discard the 2 rightmost fishing tiles, shift the rest right, then refill. Legendary Fish removed this way return to the bag instead of being discarded.
- **Gift:** each player may trigger the Gift Ability of exactly 1 Villager friend; then trigger all applicable Gift symbols on non-Villager cards.
- **Pet:** pass the Pet/Starting Player Token to the player on the left.
- **Green Crow:** choose and discard 1 crop in a green field.
- **Red Crow:** choose and discard 1 crop in a red field.
- **Event:** draw and resolve an Event Card.
- **Joja:** draw a Joja Tile and place it by the indicated action/location.
- **Shipping Bin:** players may trade and sell resources.

Field 3 counts as both red and green. Festival Cards use their printed effect.

### 4.2 Planning Phase

Players may discuss plans and freely trade resource tiles and tradable cards. Then every player puts their pawn at any location. This placement is not movement and does not allow foraging.

Declared plans are not binding. After the Action Phase begins, players may change their intended actions but cannot freely change their starting location.

### 4.3 Action Phase

Starting with the Pet Token holder and proceeding clockwise, each player completes a whole turn. Choose one turn structure:

- **Action + Action:** take 2 actions at the current location and do not move normally.
- **Action + Move + Action:** take an action, move along exactly 1 path, then take an action at the new location.

A player may decline either action. After finishing:

1. Return the pawn to the Farmhouse.
2. Choose no more than 1 type of End of Turn Effect.
3. Repeat that one selected effect as many times as its cost can be paid.

## 5. Movement and foraging

Whenever a pawn moves along a path, the player may take either 1 adjacent facedown Forageable or 1 adjacent Tree. This does not cost an action. A tile is eligible if its area shares a border with the traveled path.

- Put a normal Forageable in inventory after revealing it.
- If its icon indicates an Artifact or Mineral, discard the Forageable and draw the indicated side/type from the gray bag.
- A Tree becomes Wood when claimed.
- Trees work similarly to Forageables but are **not** Forageables for card effects.

## 6. Locations

- **Farm:** Water Crops; Collect from Animals.
- **Ranch:** Buy Animals.
- **Town:** Buy and Plant Seeds; Make a Friend; Reveal and Donate to Bundles.
- **Mountain:** Explore the Mine; Buy Buildings; Lake Fishing.
- **Forge:** Open Geodes; Donate to Museum.
- **Forest:** River Fishing.
- **Beach:** Ocean Fishing.

## 7. Farm and Ranch actions

### Water Crops — Farm

Spend 1 action to move every planted crop 1 space right. A crop leaving the track is harvested into inventory. If there is no room, first discard another resource or discard the harvested crop.

Crops do not wither at a season change. Crows can still destroy them.

### Collect from Animals — Farm

Spend 1 action and roll all 3 Animal Dice. Each die activates every owned animal matching its symbol once. Each activation produces the shown resource. For example, 2 Cow results with 2 Cows produce 4 Milk.

Happy-side animals produce Quality Animal Products.

### Buy Animals — Ranch

Spend 1 action and buy any number of affordable animals. The group must own each animal's required Coop or Barn. Pay the printed Gold cost. Animals and Buildings are shared by the group.

## 8. Town actions

### Buy and Plant Seeds

Spend 1 action at Pierre's. During the action:

- Buy any number of current-season Crop Tiles for 1 Gold each.
- Plant each purchase immediately into an empty field matching the crop's printed watering number.
- Normally, do not stack crops when planting.
- Sell any number of resources for their printed Gold values.
- Buying, planting, and selling may be interleaved in any order.

### Make a Friend

Spend 1 action and reveal the top Villager.

- Give an acceptable resource to keep the card as a friend and gain Hearts.
- Otherwise discard the Villager; the action is still spent.
- Loved gift: 2 Hearts.
- Any giftable resource not Loved or Hated: Liked, 1 Heart.
- Hated or No Gift resource: cannot be gifted.
- During the Villager's birthday season, gain 1 additional Heart.

On a Gift icon, each player chooses no more than 1 of their Villager friends and activates that friend's Gift Ability.

### Reveal and Donate to Bundles

Spend 1 action at the Community Center. During this single action, reveal/replace Bundles and donate as much as possible.

To reveal or replace a Bundle, discard Hearts equal to player count, then choose one:

- Reveal the leftmost facedown Bundle, or
- Replace a revealed Bundle with a new Bundle matching the same room.

Replacing a Bundle discards all donations already on it. Partial donations are allowed and stay on the card. When a Bundle reaches its required total, return Supply resources to the Supply, discard other donated tiles as appropriate, and remove the Bundle to show the room is restored.

Rewards:

- First 5 completed Bundles: draw 1 regular Item and give it to any player.
- Sixth/final completed Bundle: draw 1 Epic Item and give it to any player.

## 9. Forge actions

### Open Geodes

Spend 1 action to open any number of held Geodes. For each Geode:

1. Roll 1 Stardew Die.
2. Read that Geode's result from the board chart.
3. Gain the indicated Stone, Ore, Mineral, Artifact, or Item.
4. Discard the Geode, or flip it if the result turns it into Ore.

### Donate to Museum

Spend 1 action to donate any number of Artifacts and Minerals.

- Each tile must enter an empty Museum slot matching its letter A–H.
- A `?` tile can enter any empty slot.
- Gain 1 Heart per donated tile.
- Filling either full Museum column awards 1 Epic Item, up to 2 total.

## 10. Mountain actions

### Buy Buildings

Spend 1 action at Robin's. Buy any number of Buildings by paying each printed Gold and material cost. Take the purchased tiles; all their effects and ownership are shared.

### Explore the Mine

Spend 1 action and roll 2 Stardew Dice. Assign either die to the Map Card row and the other to the column; resolve their intersection.

Possible outcomes:

- Gain Stone.
- Gain Bug Meat.
- Gain available Ore.
- Gain an available Geode.
- Descend 1 Mine Level.
- Gain an Item.
- Trigger the current Mine Level's Monster Ability.
- Draw and resolve a Mine Event.

The Mine Level shows which Ores/Geodes are currently **available** and the current Monster Ability. When descending, reveal the next Mine Level and replace the Map Card. At Level 12, a further descent changes only the Map Card.

Revealing Level 12 is enough to satisfy the reach-bottom Goal.

## 11. Fishing

Fishing locations are Forest/River, Beach/Ocean, and Mountain/Lake. Spend 1 action at one of them.

### Normal catch

1. Roll all 3 Stardew Dice.
2. Only target Fish Track tiles matching the current location.
3. Assign Heart, Junimo, and Stardrop results to fish requirements.
4. Each die can be assigned to only 1 fish.
5. Catch as many fish as the 3 dice can separately satisfy.

After every Fishing Action, shift remaining tiles right and refill from the Fishing Bag, starting with the rightmost empty slot.

### Legendary Fish

They are caught normally. They return to the bag rather than being discarded if Fish Move would remove them. When sold, gifted, or donated, they are discarded normally. They are wild for fishing Bundles. A Legendary Fish Goal requires only that they were caught, not retained.

### Crab Pot Fish

During a Fishing Action, discard 1 Bug Meat per Crab Pot Fish taken from the current location. No die is required. This is in addition to the normal fishing roll.

### Trash

If the action catches no fish of any kind, the player may take 1 Trash Tile from the Track. Trash can be discarded at any time.

### Treasure Chests

When catching a fish immediately to the left of a Treasure Chest, discard the Chest and gain 1 Item.

### Fishing Bag

Fishing tiles do not return to the bag after normal use. The only rulebook exception is a Legendary Fish removed by Fish Track movement.

## 12. End of Turn Effects

After returning to the Farmhouse, choose only 1 of these effect types and repeat it as many times as affordable:

- **Build Staircase:** discard Stone equal to player count to descend once in the Mine.
- **Pet Animals:** pay the Hearts printed on an owned Animal to flip it Happy.
- **Remove Joja:** remove 1 Joja Tile by paying either 1 Heart or 5 Gold, not both.
- **Upgrade Starting Tool:** pay the resource printed on the current Tool level and advance it 1 level.

## 13. Inventory, cards, and resources

Normal per-player limits:

- 6 tiles in inventory, including ordinary resources, Wood, Stone, Fiber, Trash, and Monster Loot.
- 2 regular Items.
- 2 Profession Upgrades.
- No limit to Epic Items, friends, or retained Event Cards.

If inventory is full when gaining a tile, discard something to make room or discard the gained tile.

### Trading

Players may trade resources and tradable cards during Planning and a Shipping Bin effect. They cannot normally trade during Action Phase. Profession Upgrades cannot be traded. Epic Items can be traded unless their text says otherwise.

### Resource handling

Basic resource types are Crops, Forageables, Fish, Animal Products, Ore, Geodes, Artifacts, and Minerals. Other held tiles such as materials, Trash, and Monster Loot still occupy inventory but generally cannot be gifted.

Resources originating in the Tile Tray return there when discarded. Fish, seasonal Forageables, Artifacts, and Minerals go to discard piles unless a specific rule returns them elsewhere.

Gold and Hearts are shared. Gold, Hearts, and Stone are not limited by the supplied token count; use substitutes if necessary. Other resources are component-limited.

## 14. Starting Tools and Profession Upgrades

A Starting Tool begins at Level 0. Keep its cards stacked so the current level is faceup. Upgrading replaces the current effect with the next level.

At the end of each season, every player draws 2 Profession Upgrades matching their Player Mat and may keep 1. If already at the 2-card limit, discard an old Upgrade to make room. For a double-sided Upgrade, choose which side is active when keeping it.

Profession Gift Abilities trigger from Gift icons like other applicable non-Villager cards.

## 15. Season End Cards

When a Season End Card is drawn:

1. Remove all current Forageables and Trees.
2. Place the next season's Forageables and Trees.
3. Each player draws 2 matching Profession Upgrades and may keep 1.
4. Draw the next Season Card immediately.
5. Resolve that new Regular/Festival card, then continue to Planning and Action Phases.

Existing crops remain planted and do not wither.

## 16. Joja Tiles

A Joja icon draws a tile that modifies or blocks the matching board action. It remains until removed. Normally, remove Joja only with the End of Turn Effect by paying 1 Heart or 5 Gold. Artisan mode cannot be won while any Joja Tile remains.

## 17. Marriage

To marry:

1. Gain the Mermaid's Pendant Epic Item.
2. Attach it to a Villager friend displaying the Pendant icon.
3. Take the Spouse Pawn.

During Planning, place the Spouse at any location. During the owning player's turn, the Spouse takes 1 action at its location, before or after all of the player's normal actions. It may use that player's inventory, cards, and abilities, but cannot trigger an End of Turn Effect.

## 18. Variants

### Random Season Deck

For each season, randomly select 4 cards from all 20 available for that season and put them over its Season End Card. Festivals count as Regular Season Cards for construction.

### Easier teaching game

Start with one or more Bundles already revealed.

### Artisan scoring

Play Artisan with extra Joja-symbol cards. If the group wins, its score is the number of Joja Tiles removed.

### Short game

- Use 2 Season Cards plus the Season End Card per season.
- Reveal and complete only 2 Grandpa's Goals.
- Randomly choose 3 Community Center Rooms and use 1 facedown Bundle in each.
- Restore the Center by completing those 3 Bundles.

## 19. Solo rule

Use normal rules except for Profession Upgrades. At each Season End, draw 3 Upgrades from any combination of the 4 Profession decks, keep 1, and discard the others.

## 20. Rulebook clarifications checklist

- Crops do not wither at season changes.
- Items/resources cannot normally be traded during Action Phase.
- Trash and Fiber may be discarded at any time.
- The Pet moves only when instructed by a Season Card.
- Friendship and Tool Upgrade Goals use team totals.
- Gold and Hearts are shared.
- At Mine Level 12, descending replaces only the Map Card.
- Whenever a tile is gained, it enters inventory unless discarded.
- The game ends immediately when the selected mode's objectives are complete.
