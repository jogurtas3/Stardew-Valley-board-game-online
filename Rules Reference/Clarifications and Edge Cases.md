# Clarifications and Edge Cases

These notes supplement `Complete Rules.md`. Priority is: **specific card text > original rulebook > designer clarification > community interpretation**. Most items below come from answers by designer Cole Medeiros or a forum compilation that cites his answers. Anything less certain is explicitly labeled.

## Actions, timing, and costs

- An action is the cost of entering/using the location's activity. When an action says to do “any number” or “as much as able,” all of it occurs within that single action.
- The Community Center action can reveal, replace, donate to, and finish multiple Bundles in one action if costs can be paid.
- Opening any number of Geodes is one action.
- Buying any number of animals or Buildings is one action, but every purchased piece has its own cost.
- A Joja fee can differ by wording. A fee attached to taking the action is paid once; a cost increase attached to each purchase is paid for every purchase. Example: Small Business Tax applies once to the Geode action, while Cost Increase adds to each animal.
- Card effects may interrupt a multi-part action if their timing permits. Designer example: the Shipping Bin Building's “sell at any time” can sell crops while a large harvest is resolving.
- Paying/discarding one resource for one ability cannot simultaneously pay for or trigger a second “discard X to…” ability.
- Season Card icons resolve strictly from top to bottom. This matters when two Crows, two watering effects, or other dependent effects appear.

## Movement and foraging

- Planning Phase placement is not movement and grants no Forageable.
- A normal move or a move granted by Joja Cola, Warp Totem, or Horse allows foraging unless the effect says otherwise.
- Minecart movement explicitly replaces the normal movement/foraging option, so it does not grant a Forageable.
- A moved pawn may forage only once per qualifying move unless an effect explicitly grants more.
- Trees are never Forageables for effects that say “gain a Forageable.” A card must explicitly include Trees/Wood.
- A facedown Fiber or Stone tile is a Forageable while on the map. Once revealed, it is only a material, not a Forageable resource.

## Crops, Hoe, and Watering Can

- The no-stacking restriction is specifically a rule for **planting** crops. Effects such as Joja Water Shortage may cause crops to share a field space.
- The Hoe affects the Buy and Plant Seeds action only unless its card says otherwise. It does not modify Event Cards such as Giant Crops.
- An upgraded Watering Can modifies the player's Water Crops action. It does not increase movement caused by Rain, Evelyn, Retaining Soil, Rain Totem, Deluxe Scarecrow, or similar effects that simply say to water/move all crops once.
- If a Season Card has two watering effects, resolve both separately. Deluxe Scarecrow can therefore cause two waterings if it responds to both.
- Field 3 is both red and green. With ordered Crow icons, a crop in field 3 may be chosen for the first Crow, potentially leaving no valid crop for the second.
- Rarecrow can cancel both Crows when its wording covers plural Crows.
- A Scarecrow protecting fields 2–5 does not let the player nominate an empty protected field. If a valid crop exists in field 1 for the relevant Crow, it is eaten.
- Crops may remain planted across seasons.

## Supply, inventory, and discards

- Wood, Stone, Fiber, Trash, and Monster Loot occupy the 6-slot inventory even though they are grouped as “other tiles” rather than the 8 basic resource types.
- Gold, Hearts, and Stone are unlimited by component quantity. Track extra amounts with substitutes.
- Other resources are limited by the physical supply. If an Animal roll would produce more Milk/Eggs/etc. than remain, gain only those available.
- When an effect says **from the Supply**, use the Tile Tray.
- **Available Ore/Geode** means only types shown on the current Mine Level.
- **Any Ore/Geode** means any type in the game, still subject to component availability.
- Crops and Animal Products gained directly from the Supply are normal, not Quality, unless Quality is explicitly stated.
- Tile Tray resources return to the Tray when discarded. Seasonal tiles, Fish, Artifacts, and Minerals normally go to their respective discard piles.
- A deliberate “discard to use” effect does not trigger merely because a card was discarded to obey a hand limit.

## Grandpa's Goals and victory

- Friendship, Tool Upgrade, Animal, Building, Museum, Gold, and Legendary Fish requirements are team totals after multiplying by player count. They need not be divided evenly among players.
- The Tool Goal counts total upgrade steps, not the number of players with upgraded tools.
- The Mine Goal is complete when Level 12 is revealed. No additional descent or action on Level 12 is needed.
- Win immediately as soon as all objectives for the chosen mode are met. Do not finish the year or endure another Season Card.
- The Legendary Fish Goal remembers fish that were caught even if they were later sold, gifted, donated, or discarded.

## Bundles and Community Center

- Reveal facedown Bundles from left to right.
- Once revealed, donations can be made to any room in any order.
- Partial donations stay on the Bundle.
- Replacing a Bundle loses all donations already on it.
- Legendary Fish are wild for all fishing Bundles.
- Bundle completion rewards can be assigned to any player.
- Hearts are scarce early. A useful planning baseline for a normal game is at least 6 Hearts per player to reveal 6 rooms, plus the Bulletin Board requirement if applicable. This is strategy, not an additional rule.

## Fishing

- For rules purposes, “catch,” “gain,” and “take” a Fish Track tile are treated equivalently unless specific card text distinguishes them.
- Catching a Crab Pot Fish counts as catching a fish. Therefore, it prevents taking Trash for that Fishing Action even if the dice caught nothing.
- Catching/gaining the fish immediately left of a Treasure Chest triggers that Chest.
- A Treasure Chest Profession/Card can trigger when a Chest is gained outside the normal Fishing Action if its wording does not restrict the source.
- Two adjacent Treasure Chests do not chain under rules as written. A Chest is gained only when a fish immediately to its left is caught. Taking both is a harmless house-rule option, not the official rule.
- Bobbers that allow targeting another location still work unless the current Joja Tile blocks fishing entirely. Match the exact location restriction on the Joja Tile.
- Luremaster says “any” Crab Pot Fish, so designer clarification allows one from any Fish Track location, regardless of the fisher's current location.
- Festival of Ice tiles ignored/redrawn are discarded, not returned to the Fishing Bag. Only Legendary Fish removed by Fish Track movement return to the bag.

## Mine and Geodes

- Cherry Bomb lets the player choose and resolve 1 result space on the current Map Card. Bomb and Mega Bomb choose their printed number of result spaces on that same Map Card.
- Resolve all spaces selected by one Bomb on the same current Map Card. If one selected result descends, resolve the Staircase last so the Map Card does not change midway.
- Duplicate result spaces may be chosen if the card permits multiple selections and the selected spaces are valid.
- “Available” Ore/Geodes are restricted by current Mine Level; “any” is not.
- At Mine Level 12, descending changes only the Map Card.
- The Omni Geode Heart result is resolved left to right: gain a Mineral, then gain an Artifact.
- Miner's Treat benefits the player performing the Mine action unless card text specifies another recipient.

## Gifts, friends, and specific Gift timing

- On each Season Gift icon, each player triggers at most 1 Villager friend's Gift Ability, then all qualifying non-Villager Gift cards trigger.
- “Common Item” and lowercase “item” on first-print cards refer to the regular Item deck unless context clearly says otherwise.
- Shane: if the group owns the Coop but both Chicken tiles are already owned, gain the fallback Egg.
- Robin's and Jas's Gift Abilities are immediate free effects, not permanent permissions.
- Jas allows one player to donate one Artifact/Mineral immediately and ignore its printed Museum letter, effectively treating it as `?`.

## Profession Upgrades and selling

- Gold-increase abilities apply whenever the relevant resource is sold, including Season Shipping Bin effects and Pierre's action, unless the card limits the sale method.
- Since selling bonuses belong to a player, trade goods during Planning to the player with the best matching sale bonus.
- Profession Upgrades cannot be traded.
- Double-sided Upgrades use only the chosen face.

## Marriage

- The Mermaid's Pendant can remain unattached until its owner has a qualifying friend or trades it during Planning to a player who does.
- If transferred and attached during Planning, that player may place and use the Spouse in that same round.
- Spouse takes 1 action before or after the owning player's entire normal turn sequence, shares their inventory/cards/abilities, and never uses an End of Turn Effect.

## Card-specific notes from the clarification compilation

- **Magic Rock Candy:** “once” means the modified End of Turn Effect is performed only once rather than repeated as normal.
- **Livin' Off the Land:** “seasonal” means crops matching the current season; it has no crop result in Winter.
- **Giant Crops:** Hoe upgrades do not modify the Event.
- **Bomb/Mega Bomb:** choose all result spaces on one map; resolve a chosen Staircase last.
- **Omni Geode:** on the dual Heart result, take Mineral then Artifact.

When an individual card in the user's copy conflicts with these notes, use that card's exact wording and the printing/version of `stardew rules.pdf` first.
