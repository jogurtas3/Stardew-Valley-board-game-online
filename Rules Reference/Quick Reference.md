# Quick Reference

## Win

Standard game: complete all 4 Grandpa's Goals and 1 Bundle in each of the 6 Community Center Rooms before the Season Deck ends.

## Round

1. **Season:** Starting Player draws and resolves icons top to bottom.
2. **Planning:** trade, discuss, and place pawns anywhere. Placement is not movement.
3. **Action:** in Starting Player order, each player chooses:
   - Action + Action, or
   - Action + Move 1 path + Action.
4. Return pawn to Farmhouse; choose 1 End of Turn Effect type and repeat it as affordable.

## Season icons

- Star: flip 1 planted crop to Quality.
- Rain: move all crops 1 space right; harvest those leaving the track.
- Fish: discard 2 rightmost, shift, refill; moved Legendary Fish return to bag.
- Gift: each player triggers 1 friend; trigger all qualifying non-friend Gift cards.
- Pet: pass Starting Player Token left.
- Crow: discard a crop in matching red/green field; field 3 is both.
- `?`: Event.
- Joja: draw/place Joja Tile.
- Shipping Bin: trade and sell.

## Movement

A qualifying move allows taking either 1 adjacent Forageable or 1 adjacent Tree. Planning placement grants nothing.

## Locations/actions

- Farm: Water Crops; Collect from Animals.
- Ranch: Buy Animals.
- Town: Buy/Plant/Sell; Make a Friend; Community Center.
- Mountain: Explore Mine; Buy Buildings; Lake Fish.
- Forge: Open Geodes; Museum.
- Forest: River Fish.
- Beach: Ocean Fish.

## End of Turn Effects — choose only 1 type

- Stone × players: descend Mine.
- Printed Heart cost: flip Animal Happy.
- 1 Heart **or** 5 Gold: remove 1 Joja.
- Printed resource: upgrade Starting Tool.

## Inventory

- 6 tiles total, including materials and Trash.
- 2 Items.
- 2 Profession Upgrades.
- Unlimited Epic Items, friends, retained Events.
- Gold and Hearts are shared.
- Gold, Hearts, and Stone are not component-limited.

## Fishing reminders

- Fish only at matching River/Ocean/Lake location.
- Roll 3 Stardew Dice; each die can pay for only 1 fish.
- Bug Meat catches Crab Pot Fish in addition to the roll.
- No fish of any kind caught: may take Trash.
- Catch fish immediately left of Chest: gain Item.
- Refill rightward after every Fishing Action.

## Easily missed

- Reveal Bundles left to right; donate to any revealed Bundle.
- One Community Center action can reveal/donate as much as affordable.
- Legendary Fish are wild for fishing Bundles.
- Tool and friend Goals use team totals.
- Reaching/revealing Mine Level 12 completes the Mine Goal.
- Crops do not wither between seasons.
- Only normal movement and qualifying card movement permit foraging.
- Win immediately when all selected difficulty objectives are complete.
